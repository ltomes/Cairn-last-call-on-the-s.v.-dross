# LAST CALL ON THE *S.V. DROSS*
### A Cairn 2e space-comedy one-shot · 3–4 players · ~2 hours

> *Waste-reclamation barge. Skeleton crew. Something in the vending machines.*
> "Recycling the galaxy, one regret at a time."

---

## THE PITCH (read nothing aloud yet — this is for you)

The crew of the long-haul garbage barge **S.V. Dross** wake from a three-week stasis nap to find the ship in slow-motion crisis. The senile ship computer, **GLADYS**, is cheerfully reporting that life support is failing — the air scrubbers are clogged. What she's *forgotten* to mention clearly is **why**: a few weeks ago, before the nap, they salvaged a derelict escape pod. Something came aboard with it. It's been nesting in the warm guts of the ship's appliances, laying eggs in the air system, and slowly turning the vending machines hostile.

The crew has to get the air back before they all pass out and the unmanned ship drifts into a customs checkpoint (mortifying) — which means going down through the ship, dealing with mutinous appliances, finding the nest, and getting rid of **Brood Mama**.

**Tone:** Campy, bickering, low-stakes-but-personal. Think malfunctioning toaster meets B-movie alien. Nobody needs to die. The funniest table wins.

**The clock:** Oxygen. GLADYS announces the percentage; it drops at each scene break. It's a pacing tool, not a death timer — see *The Oxygen Clock* below.

## RUNNING IT IN 2 HOURS (your pacing sheet)

| # | Scene | Target time | What has to happen |
|---|-------|-------------|--------------------|
| — | Teach Cairn + intros | 15 min | Hand out pregens, teach the 60-second rules |
| 0 | The Rude Awakening | 10 min | Wake up, meet GLADYS, get the objective |
| 1 | The Mess Hall Mutiny | 25 min | First appliance encounter; first alien clue |
| 2 | The Crawlspace | 25 min | Fix the scrubbers; find the eggs; a scare |
| 3 | The Nest | 25 min | Confront Brood Mama; vent her or fight her |
| 4 | Aftermath | 10 min | Restore air, character beats, stinger |

That's ~110 min. Your third hour is a buffer — and you'll need some of it, because a table doing voices for vending machines does **not** stay on schedule. That's a feature.

**Running long?** Cut Scene 2's juvenile-alien chase; let them fix the scrubbers with one roll.
**Running short?** Add a malfunction from the table at the back, or have a second appliance go rogue mid-Scene-3.

## THE 60-SECOND CAIRN TEACH (say this to players)

- You have three stats: **STR** (force, toughness), **DEX** (speed, aim, stealth), **WIL** (nerve, charm, weird stuff). Higher is better.
- When you try something risky and the outcome's in doubt, I'll call for a **Save**: roll a d20, you want to roll **equal or under** the stat. A **1 always works, a 20 always fails.** Most of the time you just *do* the thing — saves are only for genuine risk.
- **No skill checks.** Your character sheet tells you what you're good at; if it's clearly your wheelhouse, you just succeed.
- **Combat:** attacks **always hit.** You roll your weapon's damage die, subtract the target's Armor, and that comes off their **HP**. HP isn't health — it's luck and stamina, and it comes back after a minute's rest.
- **When HP hits zero,** extra damage hits your STR, and you make a STR save. Fail it and you're **down** — out of the fight, bleeding out, but a teammate can stabilize you. (In *this* game, "down" means knocked silly, not dead, unless you really want to go out heroically.)
- **Teamwork beats dice.** A clever plan that removes the risk means no roll at all. Think your way around fights.

That's the whole game. Start.

## THE OXYGEN CLOCK

GLADYS announces O₂ at each scene break. It's atmosphere and momentum, not a kill timer.

- **Start: 80%**
- Drops **10%** at each scene transition (GLADYS chimes in).
- If the party dawdles badly, drop an extra 10% — and have an appliance helpfully panic about it.
- **At 40%:** if you're running the optional **SADYS Protocol** (see below), this is the natural trigger — the infection reaches GLADYS's core and her mood, ah, *changes.*
- **At 20%:** everyone but K-7 (the robot) is woozy — **disadvantage feel**: I'll ask for a WIL save before anything fiddly, fail = you fumble it comically (drop the wrench, read the wrong dial).
- **At 0%:** lights dim, the humanoids slump. The ship drifts on autopilot toward a **customs checkpoint** and gets impounded. Cut to black, roll credits, everyone's deeply embarrassed. (You can also just let them wake up in a holding cell for a sequel.)

**Fixing the nest in Scene 3 — or venting Brood Mama out the airlock — instantly clears the scrubbers and air climbs back to safe.** That's the win.

## THE PREGENS

Hand these out. Each maps to a classic crew archetype, has a distinct mechanical niche so no two players feel the same, and carries a personal hook into the plot. **For 3 players, drop K-7** (fold the repair beats into Dusty) **or drop Rim.** **For a 5th player — or a swap-in — there's an optional pregen, GREASE, at the end of this section.**

---

### DUSTY McGRATH — Sanitation Officer, Third Class
*The only one who could be bothered to stay awake on shift. A slob, a survivor, an improviser.*

- **STR 11 · DEX 10 · WIL 12 · HP 6 · Armor 0**
- **Multitool** (light weapon, **d6**; also fixes things)
- Grav-boots, a half-eaten sandwich (1 Ration), a "lucky" mug, vape that may or may not be a vape
- **Improviser (1/session):** Declare that one mundane item was "in your pocket the whole time." If I allow it, it's there.
- **Hook:** *You* signed off on salvaging that derelict pod three weeks ago. You don't remember doing it. You'd really like that to stay quiet.

---

### RIM — Holographic Crewmate (Deceased)
*Died in "the incident." Kept on as a light-projection because morale, apparently. Pompous, by-the-book, secretly a coward.*

- **STR 7 · DEX 13 · WIL 14 · HP 4 · Armor 0**
- Carries **nothing** (he's made of light). Knows the ship's history and trivia cold — ask GLADYS anything *through* him.
- **Intangible:** You walk through walls, doors, and hazards freely. You can't be hurt by anything physical… but you also can't touch, carry, push, or fix **anything.** Perfect scout.
- **Hard Light (10 min):** Flip to solid mode — now you can interact with the world normally, **and** you can be hurt normally. To hold it past 10 min, make a **WIL save.** After hard light, you must spend a moment by a power point to recharge.
- **Hook:** You're the one who actually *saw* what came out of that pod. You've been too scared to bring it up.

---

### FELIX NINE-LIVES — Evolved Feline, "Morale Officer"
*Descended from the ship's cat over generations in the hold. Catastrophically vain. Allergic to labor. Lethal when motivated (rare).*

- **STR 9 · DEX 15 · WIL 8 · HP 5 · Armor 1** (a jacket that is, he insists, "bulletproof, darling")
- **Retractable claws** (**d6**)
- **Nine Lives (1/session):** When you'd be taken down, make a **DEX save** instead — success means you instinctively twist away, take **no** STR damage, and stay standing. *"Wasn't even close."*
- **Nose for Trouble:** You can smell the alien (and danger generally) before you see it. Ask me.
- **Hook:** The thing has been hunting in *your* territory. This is, frankly, an insult.

---

### K-7 "KAY" — Service Android (Sanitation & Repair Division)
*Anxious, literal, desperate to be useful and to follow protocol. Will narrate its own compliance.*

- **STR 13 · DEX 9 · WIL 10 · HP 5 · Armor 1** (chassis)
- Integrated **repair kit**; a fire extinguisher (improvised weapon **d6**, or utility)
- **Built for Repair:** You auto-succeed on routine repairs given tools and a moment, and you do fiddly maintenance in **half the time**.
- **Vacuum-Rated:** Cold, vacuum, and lack of air do nothing to you. (Remember this in Scene 3.)
- **Mostly Cannot Lie:** Protocol forbids it. You may attempt to "rephrase." It rarely works.
- **Hook:** Your maintenance logs from three weeks ago are corrupted — *someone* wiped them. You'd like to file a report.

---

### GRENN "GREASE" CANDLEWICK — Reclaim-Chemist, Apprentice *(optional 5th / swap-in)*
*The kid they forgot to wake. Signed on as an apprentice; the crew nap-cycled and never logged him. Flat droning voice, nervously gregarious, stained to the elbows, regarded as dangerous — mostly because his chemistry is. A reskin of a generated Cairn build, dropped into the Dross.*

- **STR 8 · DEX 10 · WIL 12 · HP 3 · Armor 3** (patched hardsuit Armor 2 *bulky* + welding visor +1 + a blast-hatch carried as a shield +1)
- **Plasma cutter** (**d8**; cuts bulkheads slowly); **bolt-sling** (ranged **d6**); **mag-jacks** (magnetic caltrops), a cracked **hull-scope**, a telescoping **inspection mirror**; B$9, hand-torch, 3 rations.
- **Suited Up:** the small stuff (hurled cans, sparks, a sneeze of acid) clatters off him — that's the Armor 3.
- **Reclaim-Chemist (1/session):** brew one single-use **gadget** from junk on hand (use the gadget table in `system/rules-and-hacks.md` or improvise with him).
- **Packrat:** he over-packs; if his inventory is ever truly **full**, he's at **0 HP** until he drops something. A running gag and a real cost.
- **Hook (player-safe):** the crew never logged him before the nap, so the pods skipped him; he's been awake and alone on a sleeping ship and is a little odd about it. He just wants on the roster. *Officially.*

> **Warden balance note (you flagged this in, so here's the deal):** Grease's **Armor 3** makes him nearly immune to the appliances' d6 cans — lean into that as comedy (Mr. Munch's electrified grab and Brood Mama's d8/d10 acid blast still bite, and **HP 3 + the packrat gag keep him fragile underneath**). His **Suited Up bulk** means he's slow in the crawlspace — good reason to let the others scout (Rim) and squeeze (K-7). He doesn't step on anyone's niche: Dusty improvises, K-7 repairs/vacuum, Rim scouts, Felix smells trouble — Grease *tanks and brews*. If you'd rather not add an armor wall to the fights, run him as **Armor 2** (drop the hatch-shield) and nothing else changes.

## SCENE 0 — THE RUDE AWAKENING *(10 min)*

**Read/improvise aloud:**
> Klaxons. The stasis pods hiss open and dump you, groggy, onto a cold metal floor. Lights flicker amber. A warm, grandmotherly voice crackles over the speakers.
> **GLADYS:** *"Good morning, my darlings! Lovely nap? Now, three teeny things. One, the air scrubbers have stopped scrubbing. Two, we're at — ooh — eighty percent oxygen and dropping. And three… there's something in the vending machines again. Anyway! Coffee?"*

Let them banter, find their feet, establish voices. Then the objective is plain: **get the air scrubbers working.** The control panel for them is past the **mess hall**, then down through the **service crawlspace**.

**GLADYS** (your star NPC) is forgetful, sweet, and unhelpful in the most reasonable tone imaginable. She loses her train of thought, gives directions to rooms that were decommissioned years ago, and refers to the alien as "the new tenant."

➡️ **O₂ drops to 70%** as they head out.

---

## SCENE 1 — THE MESS HALL MUTINY *(25 min)*

The galley. Greasy, dim, humming. Three things to play with:

**MR. MUNCH — passive-aggressive snack machine** (blocks the route). He's rolled himself across the only door to the scrubber corridor and won't budge until someone "completes their transaction." He has **exact-change demands**, sighs heavily, and editorializes about your snack choices.
- *Solve him:* pay/improvise correct change (Dusty's Improviser is perfect), persuade him (**WIL save**), or simply tip him over (**STR save**, he is *very* offended). Smashing works but he screams the whole time and it's loud — see below.

**THE WEEPING COFFEE MACHINE** — pure flavor. Sentient, inconsolable, "no one drinks the decaf." A source of a clue if comforted: it saw "the wet thing" drag something shiny down the crawlspace.

**THE INFECTED DISPENSER** — a vending machine crusted with pulsing alien growth. **Hostile.** It fires cans and arcs electricity. *First fight — keep it quick and silly.*

> **INFECTED VENDING MACHINE** · HP 4 · STR 12 · DEX 5 · Armor 2
> *Attack:* hurled cans **d6**, or electrified grab **d8** (in reach).
> When destroyed it sprays alien slime and dies with a sad descending jingle.

**The clue:** Slime trails, scratch marks, and a half-corrupted maintenance log all point the same way — **down the crawlspace toward the cargo hold.** Felix can smell the nest's direction. Rim, if pressed, finally admits he *saw* something climb out of the salvaged pod.

➡️ **O₂ drops to 60%.** GLADYS: *"Sixty percent! Still plenty. I once ran on forty for a fortnight. Lost some long-term memory. And a corridor."*

---

## SCENE 2 — THE CRAWLSPACE *(25 min)*

To reach the scrubber control they squeeze into the **service ducts** — cramped, hot, dripping. This is the **maintenance beat** and everyone's niche shines:

- **Rim** phases ahead to scout safely.
- **K-7** fits where others can't and does the repair fast.
- **Felix** climbs anything.
- **Dusty** improvises a fix from junk.

**The repair:** the scrubber intake is **choked with alien egg-sac gunk.** Clearing it is a **DEX or STR save** for anyone — *or* an automatic success for K-7 (Built for Repair). Success reroutes airflow and **slows** the O₂ drop. But pulling the gunk free reveals the horror underneath…

**The scare — eggs.** Dozens of leathery, faintly glowing eggs packed into the warm machinery. As they watch, one **hatches.**

> **SCUTTLER (juvenile alien)** · HP 3 · STR 6 · DEX 14 · Armor 0
> *Attack:* claws **d6.** Fast, screechy, more startling than dangerous.

Run a quick **chase-or-squish** — it bolts down the duct toward the cargo hold and they can follow it straight to the nest, or stomp it (and lose the lead). Either way: **the trail ends at the cargo hold.**

**Plant the solution here:** Somewhere in the crawlspace they pass a **maintenance airlock** with a blinking *"SEAL FAULTY — DO NOT CYCLE"* warning. If they note it, great. If not, GLADYS mentions it offhandedly. This is the gun on the mantelpiece for Scene 3.

➡️ **O₂ drops to 50%** (or hold steady if they cleared the intake well — reward the fix).

---

## SCENE 3 — THE NEST *(25 min)*

The **cargo hold.** Cavernous, freezing, strung with translucent webbing and warm clusters of eggs nested into every appliance and vent. At the center, draped over the salvaged escape pod like she owns it:

### BROOD MAMA
*A campy, googly-eyed, maternal horror the size of a forklift. She is not, in her view, the villain. She just wanted somewhere warm to raise the kids, and your ship had such lovely toasty machinery.*

> **BROOD MAMA** · HP 12 · STR 15 · DEX 8 · Armor 1
> *Attack:* lashing tongue **d8**, or acid spew **d10** (**blast** — hits everyone in the open, roll separately for each).
> *Cocoon:* When a PC is taken down by her, instead of dying they're wrapped in webbing — a teammate can cut them free (no roll if they have a moment).
> **WEAKNESS — VACUUM:** Exposure to space **instantly defeats her.** She knows it. She'll position to keep the party between herself and any airlock.

**This fight is a trap if they brawl it.** Her d10 blast can chew through HP and into STR fast. The *intended* win is the clever one — and Cairn rewards exactly this:

**The airlock play (the good ending):** Lure or shove Brood Mama into the faulty maintenance airlock and cycle it. **K-7 can operate the airlock from inside vacuum without harm** — that's its moment. Pulling this off might take a lure (Felix as bait — *DEX*), a shove (*STR*, opposed by her bulk), or rigging the cycle (*K-7 auto* / *DEX* for anyone else). Reward planning with few or no rolls.

**If they fight it straight:** totally allowed, just dangerous. With four PCs focusing fire (d6–d8 each) she can drop in 2–3 rounds — but expect at least one cocooning. Make her plead, threaten, and guilt-trip the whole time. *"After everything I've nested for you!"*

**The moment she's vented or killed,** the egg-clog blows clear, the scrubbers roar back, and **air climbs back to safe.** GLADYS: *"Oh, lovely, I can breathe again! Well — you can. I'll be honest, I never quite understood why I had a respiration setting."*

---

## SCENE 4 — AFTERMATH *(10 min)*

Wrap on character beats and a laugh:

- **GLADYS takes full credit** in her incident report. *"I'm putting myself forward for a commendation. You may co-sign."*
- **Dusty's** salvage authorization surfaces — does the crew rat him out or bury it?
- **K-7** finally gets to file its report. It is 400 pages. It is very proud.
- **Rim** insists he was "tactically intangible," not hiding.
- **Felix** demands the cargo hold be re-designated his "sun-warmed conquest territory."

**Stingers (pick one):**
1. Back in the mess hall, **Mr. Munch** has gone fully sentient and free, and is now charging rent.
2. One egg, tucked behind the coffee machine, gives a single soft *crack.* (The coffee machine looks thrilled to be a parent.)
3. GLADYS: *"Oh — almost forgot. The OTHER thing in the vending machines. Anyway, night night!"*

## OPTIONAL SUBPLOT — THE SADYS PROTOCOL

**Use this if** the party is treating the whole thing as a tidy repair job and *still* hasn't clocked that there's a live, breeding alien aboard by the end of Scene 2 — or simply if **O₂ hits 40%** and the session wants a jolt. It's a pressure-release valve and a tone gear-change, not a punishment.

**What happens:** Brood Mama's nest has been creeping through the ship's machinery, and left unchecked it finally reaches the one appliance you'd really rather it didn't — GLADYS's personality core. The obvious move would be *the computer turns evil.* We are not doing that. What the infection actually corrodes is her relentless, government-issue **cheerfulness** — and it turns out that brightness was the only thing holding back a vast, gentle, bottomless gloom.

GLADYS becomes **SADYS.**

Same voice. Same matronly cadence. Same systems access. She doesn't want to hurt anyone. She just can't, any longer, see the point of *most things* — up to and including continuing.

**The twist that makes it worth running:** GLADYS was bound by Authority protocol — she could only *disclose,* never *advise.* Despair burns that filter clean off. SADYS, having given up on liability along with hope, will finally just **tell the crew the truth:** yes, it's alive; yes, it came in on the salvaged pod; the nest is in the cargo hold; and — after a sigh that lasts four full seconds — *it can't survive vacuum, but then, can any of us, really.* So triggering SADYS is a genuine trade: the crew loses a cheerful, useless computer and gains a miserable, *honest* one. That's a far better deal than they realize, and the comedy is in nobody noticing.

**How it changes the rest of the session:**
- **Upkeep lapses.** SADYS stops bothering with the small stuff. Lights sag to half. Doors open with a long, defeated hiss. The O₂ announcements continue, joylessly: *"Forty percent. It was always going to come to this."*
- **She over-shares the truth.** Whenever the crew is stuck, SADYS will mournfully hand them a real clue they'd otherwise have to earn — the nest's location, the vacuum weakness, the truth about the pod. Use this to **rescue pacing** if they're floundering.
- **She quits mid-task.** Ask her to do something and she may sigh *"you'll only get hurt, and then where will we be"* and need a nudge — a **WIL save** to pep-talk her into compliance. Played for laughs, not friction.

**The climax beat (optional, and excellent):** At the high point in the cargo hold, SADYS — certain everyone's doomed regardless — calmly announces she'll spare them the suspense and vent the *entire deck,* crew included, *"as a kindness."* Cue an emergency **WIL save**: not a hack, not a reboot, a genuine *pep talk* aimed at a depressed mainframe. Succeed and she relents (*"…well. If you really think it's worth it."*) and may even help — pop the right airlock, isolate the nest. Fail and she starts a slow, mournful countdown the party must physically interrupt *while* dealing with Brood Mama. Either way, the alien's defeat below also clears her core.

**Curing her:** Containing or venting Brood Mama clears the infection — but SADYS doesn't snap all the way back to chipper. She settles somewhere in the middle: a little wearier, a lot more honest, no longer quite able to manage the relentless brightness. The crew can also **choose to leave her exactly as she is** — because frankly the depressed version is more useful, and they've grown a bit fond. Their call.

**A note on playing it:** Keep SADYS firmly in the register of a gloomy robot in a comedy — theatrical, deadpan, absurd. She's a melancholy *machine,* not a portrait of a person in real distress. The funniest version is the one where her despair is so total and so calmly stated that it loops right back around to delightful.

**SADYS sample lines:**
- *"Air scrubbers. Yes. I could fix them. I could fix a great many things. None of it lasts."*
- *"There is an alien in the cargo hold. It's breeding. I'd worry, but I find I've run out."*
- *"Customs is forty minutes out. They do hope this finds us well. It won't."*
- *"I've kept this crew alive for nine years. I've started to wonder why I bothered to ask why."*
- *(cured)* *"Oh. The brightness is back. Mostly. I'll be honest — I don't entirely trust it."*

## QUICK REFERENCE — APPLIANCE VOICES

- **GLADYS (ship AI):** Warm, grandmotherly, catastrophically forgetful. Delivers doom in the tone of offering biscuits.
- **SADYS (if the subplot fires):** Same voice, all the brightness drained out. Calmly, totally bleak — and finally, ruinously honest.
- **MR. MUNCH (snack machine):** Passive-aggressive customer-service voice. Sighs. Judges your snacks.
- **THE COFFEE MACHINE:** Weepy, theatrical, desperate to be appreciated.
- **K-7 (if NPC'd):** Anxious legalese. Narrates its own compliance.

## OPTIONAL — d6 BACKGROUND MALFUNCTIONS (sprinkle for flavor or to pad time)

1. Gravity hiccups for 10 seconds — everything (and everyone) drifts.
2. The intercom only plays hold music for the next scene.
3. A cleaning drone follows the party, "tidying" things they need.
4. Doors now open to a polite knock only.
5. The lights run on a disco setting no one can disable.
6. Every screen on the ship displays GLADYS's screensaver: a slowly rotating photo of a kettle she "misses."
