## HOW CAIRN WORKS — 60 SECONDS

- You have three stats: **STR** (force, toughness), **DEX** (speed, aim, stealth), **WIL** (nerve, charm, weird stuff). Higher is better.
- Risky thing, uncertain outcome → the Warden calls a **Save**: roll a **d20, equal or under** the stat. **A 1 always works, a 20 always fails.** Most of the time you just *do* things — saves are only for real risk.
- **No skill checks.** Your card says what you're good at; if it's clearly your thing, you just succeed.
- **Combat: attacks always hit.** Roll your weapon's damage die, subtract the enemy's Armor, the rest comes off their **HP**. HP isn't health — it's luck and stamina, back after a minute's rest.
- **Out of HP?** Damage hits your STR and you make a STR save. Fail = you're **down** (knocked silly / webbed up), not dead — a teammate can get you back up.
- **Teamwork beats dice.** A clever plan that removes the risk means no roll at all. Outthink the problem.
