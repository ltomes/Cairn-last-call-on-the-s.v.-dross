Deck Maps

Warden reference for the three scene locations. The route runs down: stasis → mess hall → crawlspace → cargo hold.

Ship overview · the descent

How to use it

One clean vertical line. Every scene transition is a deck down and another −10% O₂ (GLADYS chimes it). If the table dawdles, drop an extra 10% and have an appliance panic about it.

The whole map is the gun on the mantel: the only thing that ends it is vacuum in the cargo hold. Everything above just gets them down there, woozier and funnier.

O₂ ≤ 20%: everyone but K-7 is woozy (WIL save before anything fiddly). At 40%, optionally trigger SADYS.

Scene 1 · the Mess Hall mutiny

Three things to play with

Mr. Munch has rolled across the only door. Pay/improvise exact change (Dusty), persuade (WIL), or tip him (STR — he is very offended). Smashing works but he screams.

The coffee machine is pure flavor — comfort it and it gives the clue: it saw “the wet thing” drag something shiny down the crawlspace.

The infected dispenser — HP 4, Armor 2, hurled cans d6 / electric grab d8. Keep it quick and silly. Slime trails point down the crawlspace.

Exit O₂ → 60%.

Scene 2 · the Crawlspace

The maintenance beat

Everyone's niche shines: Bartleby phases ahead to scout, K-7 fits + repairs fast, Crown & Whisker climbs anything, Dusty improvises a fix from junk.

The repair: clear the egg-sac gunk from the scrubber intake (DEX or STR save; K-7 auto). Success slows the O₂ drop — reward it by holding at 50%.

The scare: pulling the gunk reveals dozens of eggs; one hatches into a Scuttler. Chase-or-squish — the trail ends at the cargo hold either way.

Plant the airlock. The faulty maintenance airlock (“DO NOT CYCLE”) is the win condition for Scene 3. If they don't clock it, GLADYS mentions it offhandedly.

Scene 3 · the Nest the finale

The airlock play — the good ending

Lure or shove Brood Mama into the airlock and cycle it. K-7 can operate it from inside vacuum unharmed — that's its moment. Lure (Crown & Whisker, DEX), shove (STR vs her bulk), or rig the cycle (K-7 auto / DEX). Reward planning with few or no rolls.

If they brawl it: allowed but dangerous — her d10 blast chews HP into STR. Four PCs focusing fire drop her in 2–3 rounds; expect a cocooning. Make her plead and guilt-trip the whole time.

Win: vented or killed, the egg-clog blows clear, scrubbers roar back, air climbs to safe. She positions to keep the party between herself and any airlock — she knows.

Cargo hold is freezing & airless-adjacent — remember K-7 is Vacuum-Rated.
