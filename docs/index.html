Last Call on the S.V. Dross

A Cairn 2e space-comedy one-shot. Waste-reclamation barge. Skeleton crew. Something in the vending machines.

For players
🪪

Crew Builder Player-safe

Pick one of the four pregens, tweak the name, stats, and kit, then print or save your card. Or roll a fresh recruit.
🎲

How to play

Three stats (STR/DEX/WIL). Roll d20 equal or under to save. Attacks always hit — roll damage, subtract Armor. Teamwork beats dice. The Crew Builder card has the rest.

Table Display — live control Warden

Your game-night second screen: push room art, deck maps, ambience & SFX, NPC voices, an O₂ countdown, and a posable 3D deck view to a TV from your phone. Open table/display.html on the TV; a buildable Android TV app lives in tv-app/. Needs the table server (docs/table/server.mjs) running on your LAN — see docs/table/README.md; not active on the static GitHub Pages site.
🔊

NPC Soundboard Warden

Tap-to-speak GLADYS, SADYS, Mr. Munch and the rest in your browser's own voice. No internet needed once loaded.
🗺️

Deck Maps Warden

Deck plans rendered from the ship model, hull-conformed rooms with the waypoint guides and the airlock play marked. Player/Warden toggle. Print-friendly.
🛰️

3D Decks (interactive) Warden

Orbitable 3D space — the decks stacked as layers (A command on top → D cargo), rooms as boxes, corridors as low slabs, the hull envelope per deck. Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom. Built live from the current maps. Static renders + textured model on the 3D views page.

📐

Hull Side Elevation Warden

Orthographic profile — the four decks as stacked layers (A command on top → D cargo into the aft bulb), the hammerhead bridge forward and the external maw. Derived live from the ship model.
🖼️

Room Images Warden

AI flavour images of each room — SD 3.5 + Depth ControlNet conditioned on the conformed geometry, prompted with each room's appliances, set-dressing, and waypoint. Browse by deck.
