Rules & Hacks

⚠ WARDEN — contains spoilers

THE SYSTEM — Cairn 2e, Reskinned for the Dross

This one-shot runs on Cairn 2e by Yochai Gal (free at https://cairnrpg.com). You don't need to have read it. Players need the 60-second teach (in players/pregens.md); the Warden needs this page plus the adventure.

Everything below is either a one-line recap of base Cairn or a HACK — a deliberate deviation for this game. The hacks are the part that makes the Dross feel like a grimy space comedy instead of fantasy dungeon-crawling.

CORE RESOLUTION (base Cairn, recap)

  • Three attributes: STR, DEX, WIL. Higher is better.
  • Saves: roll d20, equal or under the attribute. 1 always succeeds, 20 always fails. Only roll when the outcome is genuinely in doubt and the stakes are real — otherwise the character just does it.
  • No skill checks. A character's background/role is their license. If it's clearly their wheelhouse, no roll.
  • Combat — attacks auto-hit. Roll the weapon's damage die, subtract the target's Armor, the rest comes off HP.
  • HP is not health. It's luck and stamina. It comes back after a moment's rest.
  • Critical Damage: when damage drops HP below 0, the overflow hits STR; the target then makes a STR save or goes down. (See the Comedy Death hack for how "down" works here.)
  • Inventory: 10 slots (4 on body, 6 in pack). Bulky = 2 slots. We don't sweat encumbrance in a 2-hour game; track it only if it's funny.

THE HACKS

1. Comedy Death — "Down, Not Dead" (HACK)

Failing a Critical Damage save does not kill a PC. They're knocked silly, webbed up, or comically incapacitated — out of the scene until a teammate spends a moment to rouse/free them (no roll). A player may opt in to a heroic death if the moment is too good to pass up. NPCs and appliances still go down for good. Why: keeps a comedy one-shot from grinding to a halt over a bad d20.

2. Hologram (HACK — trait)

A holographic character (e.g. Bartleby "Feathers") is Intangible by default: walks through walls, doors, and hazards; cannot be physically harmed; but cannot touch, carry, push, or repair anything. They can flip to Hard Light for ~10 minutes (interact and be harmed normally); holding it longer needs a WIL save, and afterward they recharge at a power point. Why: a fantastic scout and running gag, with a real cost.

3. Vacuum-Rated (HACK — trait)

A character with this (e.g. K-7) ignores vacuum, cold, and lack of air entirely. This is the literal key to the airlock solution in the finale. Why: gives the robot a defining, plot-relevant niche.

4. The Oxygen Clock (HACK — environment)

A visible countdown that drives pace. Starts at 80%, drops 10% per scene (faster if the party dawdles). The Warden announces it in the ship AI's voice. - 40%: trigger point for the optional SADYS Protocol (see adventure). - 20%: everyone not Vacuum-Rated is woozy — a WIL save before anything fiddly, fail = a comedic fumble. - 0%: humanoids slump, ship drifts into customs, impounded. (A loss state, not a death.) - Reset: clearing the nest / venting the alien restores air to safe. Why: a soft ticking clock keeps a banter-heavy table moving without threatening a TPK.

5. Pep-Talk Saves (HACK — social)

Some obstacles are emotional, not physical — most of all the ship's AI. Convincing a despairing mainframe (or a barricaded vending machine) to cooperate is a WIL save, framed as persuasion/morale rather than a hack. Why: makes social/emotional play mechanically real and very on-tone.

6. Tech, Not Magic (HACK — gadgets)

There are no spells or spellbooks. Instead, gadgets: single-purpose devices, 1 inventory slot each. A finicky or overtaxed gadget may add a Fatigue (a slot-filling jam/cooldown) on a failed WIL save, the same way casting strains a caster in base Cairn. Optional starting/loot gadgets:

d6 Gadget Effect
1 Spanner-9000 Auto-fixes one simple mechanical problem per scene.
2 Pocket Holo-Decoy Projects a convincing fake crewmate; enemies need a DEX save to ignore it.
3 Static Mop Discharges to stun one appliance/machine, once per scene.
4 Emergency Re-Breather 10 minutes of air in vacuum or low-O₂.
5 Morale Klaxon Once: the whole party rerolls one failed save (you blast the company anthem).
6 Adhesive Foam Canister Glues a door or foe in place; a STR save breaks free.

DAMAGE & ARMOR QUICK TABLE

  • Light weapon / claws / improvised: d6
  • Solid weapon / electrified grab: d8
  • Heavy / acid spew (blast): d10 — blast hits everyone in the open; roll separately for each.
  • Armor: jacket/chassis 1; reinforced casing 2. Armor subtracts from each hit.

OPTIONAL: FATIGUE & DEPRIVATION (if you want more grit)

Base Cairn rules apply if you want them: a Deprived PC (here: low on air) can't recover HP; stay Deprived a while and you add a Fatigue item, clogging a slot. For a light 2-hour comedy you can ignore this entirely and just use the Oxygen Clock — included for Wardens who like the screws tighter.
